# 🌈 Super Obby Adventure

A colorful 2D obby platformer with **50 stages**, coins, a store, and revives!

## 🎮 How to Play

- **⬅ ➡ or A / D** — Run
- **⬆ / W / Space** — Jump
- Reach the 🏁 checkered flag to finish a stage and earn **🪙 10 coins**!

## 🗺️ Stages

| Stages | Level |
|--------|-------|
| 1–10   | 🌱 Beginner |
| 11–40  | 🔥 Intermediate |
| 41–50  | 💎 Pro |

Every stage gets longer and harder — with spikes, lava, moving platforms,
bounce pads, and vanishing ghost platforms!

## 💖 Revives

If you fall with **no revive**, you go all the way back to **Stage 1**!
Buy a **💖 Revive (25 coins)** in the store to pop back at your last 🚩 checkpoint instead.

## 🛒 Store

Spend your coins on cool stuff:

- 💖 **Revive** — 25 coins
- 👟 **Speed Shoes** — 100 coins
- 🌀 **Super Jump** — 150 coins
- 🥷 **Ninja Headband** — 80 coins
- 🌈 **Rainbow Trail** — 120 coins
- ⭐ **Star Pet** — 180 coins
- 👑 **Golden Crown** — 200 coins

Your coins, items, and stage progress are saved automatically! 💾

Open `index.html` in any browser, or play it on GitHub Pages!
